## Brand & Style

This design system expresses quiet architectural luxury, tranquil retreat, and bespoke hospitality. It caters to discerning global travelers, wellness seekers, and design-conscious patrons who appreciate understated elegance over loud ornamentation. The emotional response evokes calm reassurance, privacy, refined tactility, and effortless indulgence.

The aesthetic philosophy draws on **Warm Editorial Minimalism** paired with **Contemporary Tactile Sophistication**:
- **Atmospheric Space:** Intentional, generous margins and airy section divisions replicate high-end architectural monographs and editorial print magazines.
- **Organic Warmth:** Surfaces reject clinical pure whites and cold greys, relying instead on warm ivory, alabaster, stone, and aged parchment tones.
- **Editorial Contrast:** Crisp, poised serif display typography balances against functional, optically neutral sans-serif UI elements.
- **Restrained Materiality:** Translucent champagne tints, ultra-fine boundary strokes, and diffused warm ambient shadows ground interactive modules with tactile weightlessness.

## Colors

The color palette centers around warm stone, deep mineral pigment, and muted metallic champagne luster.

### Palette Architecture
- **Primary (`#2B2623`):** Deep Umber Charcoal. Used for primary headlines, prominent interactive states, and anchored navigation framing. Provides soft optical depth without the starkness of pure black.
- **Secondary (`#B8976C`):** Aged Champagne Gold. Deployed for luxury highlights, selective focus indicators, badges, selected dates, and delicate accent lines.
- **Tertiary (`#C5A880`):** Warm Sand Brass. Used for subtle hover states, tertiary accents, and secondary iconography.
- **Neutral Light Base (`#FAF8F5`):** Warm Ivory Alabaster canvas. Paired with `#F5F2EB` (Sun-dried Linen) for secondary container nesting and card backdrops.
- **Borders & Dividers (`#E8E3DC`):** Muted Sandstone line art, providing structure without competing with photography or typography.
- **Text & Contrast:** Primary text renders in `#1A1A1A` on light canvases; muted secondary metadata uses `#6E6760`.

## Typography

The type system balances evocative editorial storytelling with effortless transaction legibility.

- **Headings & Display:** `Playfair Display` evokes timeless hospitality, classical architecture, and curated lifestyle publishing. Display weights are kept light to medium (`400` to `500`) to maintain sophistication without bulk.
- **Body & Controls:** `Plus Jakarta Sans` provides crisp, geometric clarity at smaller viewports and within complex booking matrices.
- **Microcopy & Eyebrows:** The `label-caps` style is rendered strictly in uppercase with wide tracking (`0.15em`) for resort tags, suite categories, and section markers.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is constructed on an expansive 12-column responsive fluid grid with deliberate structural framing:

- **Desktop (1200px+):** 12 columns, `4rem` outer margins, `1.5rem` gutters. Section stacks breathe with `space-xl` gaps. Max content container is capped at `1440px`.
- **Tablet (768px – 1199px):** 8 columns, `2.5rem` outer margins, `1.25rem` gutters. Editorial hero modules collapse from asymmetric split screens into balanced 2-row stacks.
- **Mobile (<768px):** 4 columns, `1.25rem` outer canvas padding, `1rem` gutters. Interactive cards expand edge-to-edge or rely on horizontal carousels with peek overflow.

Whitespace is treated as a core design element rather than passive margin; photography modules feature variable aspect ratios (16:9, 4:5, 1:1) framed inside quiet borders.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses tonal surface layering and warm atmospheric shadows rather than clinical grey drops:

- **Level 0 (Base Canvas):** Natural Warm Ivory (`#FAF8F5`). Flat foundation.
- **Level 1 (Inset & Recessed):** Sun-dried Linen (`#F5F2EB`). Used for amenity cards, datepicker wells, and input fields with a 1px border (`#E8E3DC`).
- **Level 2 (Floating Cards & Modals):** Pure Alabaster surfaces elevated via dual ambient diffusion:
  `box-shadow: 0 4px 20px -2px rgba(43, 38, 35, 0.04), 0 12px 32px -4px rgba(43, 38, 35, 0.08);`
- **Level 3 (Luxury Booking Bar & Sticky Navigation):** Frosted glass surface using `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)` on `rgba(250, 248, 245, 0.88)` with a hairline baseline border (`#E8E3DC`).
- **Focus & Selection:** Accented with an interior 1px champagne ring (`#B8976C`) accompanied by an outer soft halo (`rgba(184, 151, 108, 0.18)`).

## Shapes

The architectural shape language remains controlled, subtle, and structured:
- Containers, property visual frames, dialogs, and cards use soft, refined rounding (`0.25rem` to `0.5rem`) that honors architectural stone and masonry lines.
- **Pill Exception:** Status indicators, availability chips, category tags, and primary action buttons within booking flows leverage full rounded pill geometries (`9999px`) to create an inviting, human touch.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Deep Umber (`#2B2623`) solid fill with Ivory (`#FAF8F5`) text. Fully pill-rounded (`9999px`) or refined soft rect (`0.25rem`). Padding: `0.875rem 2rem`. Text in `label-md`. Hover shifts background to `#3F3935` with subtle elevation.
- **Secondary / Gold:** Outline style with 1px border (`#B8976C`) and `#2B2623` typography. Hover transitions to `#F5F2EB` tint with `#B8976C` text.
- **Ghost:** Warm sand hover state, 0px border, uppercase tracked label with an animated underline transition.

### Chips & Badges
- Pill-shaped (`9999px`), height 28px, padding `0.25rem 0.875rem`.
- Background in translucent champagne (`rgba(184, 151, 108, 0.12)`) paired with `#2B2623` text in `label-caps`.

### Input Fields & Selectors
- Background `#F5F2EB`, border 1px `#E8E3DC`, rounded `0.25rem`.
- Active focus state replaces border with `#B8976C` and a diffuse champagne aura. Labels sit above in `label-caps` muted grey-brown (`#6E6760`).

### Checkboxes & Radios
- Radios are circular rings with a delicate `#B8976C` center jewel on select.
- Checkboxes feature a 1px border (`#E8E3DC`) filling with `#2B2623` and a warm ivory checkmark upon activation.

### Cards & Suites Presentation
- Editorial framing: visual canvas features 1px solid `#E8E3DC` borders with generous interior padding (`space-md`).
- Image containers carry a quiet `4:5` or `16:10` ratio with smooth image zoom on container hover.

### Specialized Hospitality Components
- **Floating Booking Widget:** Horizontally partitioned bar containing Check-in/Check-out, Guests, and Destination modules separated by 1px vertical `#E8E3DC` hairlines, backed by frosted `#FAF8F5` glass.
- **Amenity Icon Clusters:** Minimal 1px stroke linear icons tinted in `#B8976C` coupled with `caption` descriptions.
