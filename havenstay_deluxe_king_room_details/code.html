Spatial Philosophy

Conceived for profound silence and sensory calm.

The Deluxe King Room is designed as an acoustic and architectural sanctuary. Wrapped in bespoke solid white oak millwork and tactile lime-wash wall treatments, every surface softens ambient reverberation to create a quiet cocoon high above the city center.

Subtle Mediterranean influences emerge in the hand-selected porous travertine bedside tables and custom terracotta accent pottery, while Scandinavian restraint defines the generous uncluttered spatial lines. Acoustic-glazed double curtain facades guarantee undisturbed restoration at any hour of the day.
